About the Event

Where ideas collide.

Tech Clash: The Tech Debate is an IOTech Club event designed for students who enjoy technology, discussion and challenging conventional thinking.

Participants will go head-to-head on thought-provoking technology topics, presenting arguments, challenging opposing viewpoints and defending their position.

The focus isn't simply on speaking. Participants are expected to think critically, use factual evidence and respond effectively when their ideas are challenged.

How It Works

Event Format

01

Get Your Topic

Participants will receive their assigned debate topic and position.

02

Make Your Case

Present your arguments within the allotted time using facts and logical reasoning.

03

Defend Your Stand

Respond to questions from opponents and judges while defending your viewpoint.

Important

Rules & Guidelines

  • Each participant gets 5 minutes per round.
  • Arguments must be supported by factual evidence.
  • No degrading or disrespectful language.
  • Participants must stay relevant to the assigned topic.
  • Judges may ask questions during the debate.
  • Judges' decision will be final.
